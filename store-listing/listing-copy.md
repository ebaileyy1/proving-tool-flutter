## Full description (max 4000 characters)
Prove It is an internal app for logging
and tracking operational trials from planning through to outcome.

Key features:
- Create and track trials with type, terminal, status, and key dates
- Record attendees, pre-trial plans, and expected outcomes
- Attach drawings and evidence photos — straight from the camera or your
  files
- Log observations and replies as a trial progresses
- Get a clear "Today" and "This week" view of upcoming trials, with a quick
  prompt to log the outcome once a trial's date arrives
- Export a full PDF report of any trial, or a CSV of your filtered trial
  list
- Works fully offline — create and edit trials with no signal, and they
  sync automatically once you're back online

This app is for authorised staff and contractors only and requires an
account.
