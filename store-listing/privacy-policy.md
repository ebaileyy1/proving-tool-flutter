# Privacy Policy — Prove It

**Last updated:** [DATE]

Prove It is an internal application used to log, track, and report on
operational trials. It is not available to the general public and is only
used by authorised staff and contractors.

## Information we collect

When you create an account and use the app, we collect:

- **Account information**: your email address and a username you choose.
- **Trial data you enter**: trial names, descriptions, outcomes, attendee
  names and companies, dates, and related notes.
- **Files you attach**: photos, drawings, and documents you upload as
  evidence for a trial.
- **Basic account activity**: when your account was created, and whether
  you've read in-app notifications.

We do not collect location data, contacts, advertising identifiers, or any
analytics/tracking data. We do not use the camera or photo library for
anything other than letting you attach a photo to a trial when you choose to.

## How we use this information

Data you enter is used solely to operate the app: storing and displaying
trials, notifying relevant team members of updates, and generating PDF
reports you request. It is shared only with other authorised users within
your organisation who have access to the same account system — never sold,
never shared with third parties for advertising, and never used for any
purpose beyond running this internal tool.

## Where data is stored

Data is stored in a cloud database ([Supabase](https://supabase.com)) and
associated cloud file storage. [UPDATE THIS SECTION if the hosting
provider/region changes.] The app also keeps a local copy of your
in-progress work on your device so it continues to work without an internet
connection, syncing automatically once you're back online.
